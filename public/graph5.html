Bezier Curve: Animated X (Time), Y (Value), and Curve (Offline)

This demo animates:
- X-Axis (Time): 0-8 (full) → slide left (-2 to 6) → sparse 0-10.
- Y-Axis (Value): 0-5 (full) → slide up (1 to 6) → sparse 0-10.
- Curve: Grows from flat to full shape in each phase.
Loops every ~8s. All elements labeled. Works offline with local D3.js.
